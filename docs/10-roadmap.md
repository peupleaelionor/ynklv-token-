## 10.5 — MVP DEFINITION

### The Minimum Viable YNKLV Ecosystem

What must exist for the first 100 users to have a complete, meaningful YNKLV experience?

**MVP Stack:**
1. **YNKLVToken.sol** — deployed, audited, locked
2. **YNKLV Pass** — mintable, dynamic, visible on OpenSea
3. **YNKLV Studio (v1)** — create profile, upload one product type (digital download), accept YNKLV payment, receive revenue automatically
4. **YNKLV Academy (v1)** — one learning track (Web3 Foundations), completion credential
5. **Wallet connection** — MetaMask + WalletConnect + Privy (embedded)
6. **EPS system (v1)** — basic hold + activity scoring, visible on dashboard
7. **Website** — fully live, all core pages
8. **Community** — Discord, Telegram, governance forum
9. **Transparency** — Treasury dashboard, audit published
10. **Fiat on-ramp** — Moonpay (international) + one mobile money integration

**What is NOT MVP:**
- YNKLV AI (Month 4+)
- Creator Tokens (Month 5+)
- YNKLV Name Service (Month 6+)
- Native mobile app (Month 7+)
- Full DAO governance (Year 2+)

## 10.6 — LAUNCH PRIORITIES

Ranked by strategic importance:

**Priority 1: Trust infrastructure before marketing**
Audits published. Treasury visible. Legal structure complete. This is non-negotiable. No launch without this.

**Priority 2: Creator onboarding before user onboarding**
Creators bring audiences. 50 great creators on day one are worth more than 50,000 casual users. Invest disproportionately in creator experience.

**Priority 3: French language before perfect English**
Francophone Africa and diaspora is the primary cultural market. The French experience must be perfect from day one.

**Priority 4: Mobile money integration before crypto exchange listings**
Getting on Binance is meaningless if a Congolese musician can't convert her YNKLV back to francs. Fiat rails serve real users. Exchange listings serve speculators.

**Priority 5: Community depth before community size**
100 deeply engaged community members are the foundation of 100,000. Scale without depth collapses.

### What Would Cause These Numbers to Fail

1. **Trust failure:** A security incident, a transparency failure, or a founding team failure
2. **Product failure:** The creator tools are not actually better than alternatives
3. **Regulatory action:** Unexpected hostile regulation in a key market
4. **Mobile money failure:** Fiat ramps not available in key markets
5. **Cultural mismatch:** The brand feels foreign, extractive, or inauthentic to its target audience

### What Would Cause These Numbers to Exceed Expectations

1. **A viral cultural moment:** One major Afrobeats artist embraces YNKLV publicly
2. **Mobile money success:** Seamless conversion from Orange Money or Wave drives mass adoption
3. **Enterprise breakthrough:** A major African company integrates YNKLV credentials
4. **AI quality:** The AI tools become genuinely best-in-class for African creative contexts
5. **Regulatory clarity:** A key African government endorses blockchain-based professional credentials
